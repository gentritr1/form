# FORM
A fictional digital sculpture exhibition created as a portfolio demonstration. Visitors explore three original mathematical surfaces, compare materials, and create a sculpture study in a live studio. A dedicated touch handle and accessible sliders control surface twist; curated surprises change the object, material, lighting, and artwork background. Visitors can type a word and FORM casts a sculpture from it (the same word always gives the same sculpture), refine it, download a 1200 × 1500 PNG poster, or copy a link that reopens exactly that sculpture. An original generated still life is presented separately from the live collection. No real client or commercial claims. All runtime resources are local; no accounts, forms that submit data, tracking, or third-party requests.
