# FORM
A fictional digital sculpture exhibition created as a portfolio demonstration. Visitors explore three original mathematical surfaces, compare materials, and create a sculpture study in a live studio. A dedicated touch handle and accessible sliders control surface twist; curated surprises change the object, material, lighting, and artwork background. Visitors can name their study and download a 1200 × 1500 PNG poster. An original generated still life is presented separately from the live collection. No real client or commercial claims. All runtime resources are local; no accounts, forms that submit data, tracking, or third-party requests.
